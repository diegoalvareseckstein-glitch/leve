Frequently asked questions

Does Leve tell me what dose to take?

No. Leve only organizes the treatment your doctor prescribed. It never recommends, prescribes or changes doses.

Is my health data safe?

Yes. Right now everything is stored only on your phone, not on our servers. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Leve runs in your browser and can be added to your home screen like an app, without taking up space.

Does it work with compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide?

Yes. You enter the dose your prescriber gave you, weekly or daily.

Are the workouts safe for beginners?

Yes. Your plan starts at your level, is low-impact when needed, and avoids exercises that load painful joints. Always check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program.
